Transcribe

Speech → text

Local Whisper transcription to SRT, WebVTT, plain text, or ELAN .eaf. Nothing is uploaded — audio never leaves your machine.

Archive Check

Format readiness

Inspect codecs and containers, flag them against DANS preferred formats, and convert to a preservation master.

A folder is scanned recursively (up to 500 media/image files).

Scrub Metadata

Strip hidden data

Find and remove EXIF, GPS, and embedded tags before you share. Images are rebuilt pixel-only; audio/video keep streams but drop all tags.

Chunkle

Cut long recordings into pieces

Split a long video or audio file into pieces of a fixed length. Every piece is named after where it starts, and a chunks.csv records its exact start and end in the original, so ELAN annotations can be moved back onto the full recording.

How to cut
Fast copies the video without re-encoding and cuts at the nearest keyframe, so pieces can be a fraction of a second longer or shorter; the manifest records the real times. Exact cuts on the second and re-encodes to MP4 (H.264), which is slower but gives smaller files that fit MaskAnyone's 200 MB upload limit more easily.
The pieces go into a new folder next to the recording; the original is not changed.

Frame rate · prototype

Stop annotations from drifting

Phone and webcam recordings often have a variable frame rate, which makes annotations slowly drift out of sync in ELAN. Check a file, and convert it to a constant frame rate if needed.

Sync · prototype

Align two recordings

Two cameras, or a camera and a separate audio recorder, rarely start at the same moment. Sync listens to both and finds the offset from the sound (a clap at the start helps). Then it can trim the earlier recording so both start together.

Uses the first 3 minutes of sound from each file.

Redact · prototype

Remove names from transcripts

Masking a voice does not change what people say. Replace names, places and other identifying words in SRT, WebVTT, plain-text or ELAN files. Suggestions are capitalised words worth checking; you decide what goes. Always read the result.

Unchunkle · prototype

Merge piece annotations into one file

After annotating Chunkle pieces in ELAN, save each piece's .eaf next to it with the same name (e.g. session03_part02_00h10m00s.eaf). Unchunkle shifts every annotation by its piece's start time and writes one .eaf for the full recording.

Agreement · prototype

Compare two coders

Inter-rater agreement between two ELAN files: Cohen's kappa over time (in 100 ms steps, with "no annotation" as its own category), and how many annotations match between coders.
